Räume und Equipment sind sorgfältig zu behandeln und in dem Zustand zu verlassen, in dem sie übernommen wurden. Schäden sind sofort zu melden; schuldhaft verursachte Schäden werden berechnet. GEMA) selbst verantwortlich, soweit im Angebot nichts anderes vereinbart ist.

Die Community-Mitgliedschaft ist kostenlos und kann jederzeit durch Löschen des Profils beendet werden. Die Artist-Membership wird nur nach Bewerbung und Zusage vergeben. The prices shown or offered at the time of booking apply. Direct bookings are paid in advance via Stripe; confirmed requests are paid on site or by invoice.

Studio sessions can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the start; later cancellations or no-shows are charged in full. For Bunker spaces, the cancellation terms of the offer apply. If we have to cancel, we refund all payments in full.

Treat rooms and equipment with care and leave them as you found them. Report damage immediately; culpably caused damage will be charged. No smoking. Follow our team's instructions. For events you are responsible for permits and registrations (e.g. GEMA) unless agreed otherwise.

Community membership is free and ends when you delete your profile. Artist membership is granted by application only, runs monthly and can be cancelled at any time effective at the end of the current billing month. Member benefits such as discounts, free tickets and Members day are personal and non-transferable; free tickets are subject to availability.

§ 8 Right of withdrawal

Consumers generally have a 14-day right of withdrawal for contracts concluded online, including the Artist membership. There is no right of withdrawal for studio sessions and space use on a fixed date (§ 312g (2) no. 9 BGB).
